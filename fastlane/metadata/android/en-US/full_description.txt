Orbit Maps shows maps, searches places and plans routes entirely on your device, from region packs you download once.

<b>Private</b>
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers
• No Google Play Services or Firebase
• Your location and searches never leave your phone

<b>Offline</b>
• Map, search and routing all work without a connection
• Download only the regions you need

<b>Open</b>
• Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)
• Free software: GPL-3.0-or-later
• Source code: https://github.com/orbitmaps-oss/orbitmaps

Orbit Maps is in early development.
